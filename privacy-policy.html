BuzzyLetters Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 19, 2026
BuzzyLetters is developed and operated by Samantha Lynch (FarmLedger).

The short version: BuzzyLetters does not collect any personal information — from children or from anyone else. The app works completely offline, has no accounts, shows no ads, and includes no analytics or tracking. Game progress stays on your device and never leaves it.

Information we collect

We collect no personal data through the BuzzyLetters app. Specifically:

  • There are no accounts and no sign-in. We never ask for a name, email address, or any other personal information inside the app.
  • The app contains no advertising and no ad SDKs.
  • The app contains no analytics, crash-reporting, or tracking tools.
  • The app includes no third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • The app works fully offline and does not send any information to us or to anyone else over the internet.

Game progress stored on your device

The only information the app keeps is game progress — things like stars and tickets earned and which stops on the learning path have been completed. This progress is stored only on your own device, in the app's private storage. It never leaves the device, is never transmitted to us, and we cannot see it. If you delete the app, this progress is deleted with it.

Children's privacy

BuzzyLetters is designed for young children who are learning to read. Because the app collects no personal information at all — from children or from adults — there is no children's data for us to store, use, or share. This approach is consistent with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA): we do not collect personal information from children under 13, so no parental consent for data collection is required. The app has no chat, no social features, no external links for children, and no advertising. Areas of the app that involve purchases are intended for parents and grown-ups.

Subscriptions and purchases

If a subscription or other purchase is offered in the app, it is processed entirely by Google Play. Google handles the payment, and we never receive or have access to your payment details, such as credit card numbers. Google's handling of purchase information is covered by Google's own privacy policy. We receive only the kind of general, non-identifying confirmation from Google that a subscription is active, so the app can unlock the paid content.

Sharing of information

We do not collect personal information, so there is nothing for us to sell, rent, or share with third parties.

Changes to this policy

If we ever change how the app handles information, we will update this policy and change the effective date at the top. If a future version of the app were to collect any information, this policy would be updated to describe it before that version is released.
